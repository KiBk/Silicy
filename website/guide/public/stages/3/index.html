Burgio — bells and green ceramics

The story: Burgio has long traditions of bronze bell-making and decorated majolica. Its ceramics often use green and sandy colours, with flowers and birds among the motifs.

Notice: Stone doorways, ceramic details and any open workshops. The ceramics museum is an option if you want more than a quick town stop.

Ride note: Town-area stop; workshop and museum access must be checked locally.

02

Santo Stefano Quisquina — the mountain pause

The story: The town sits in the Sicani mountain landscape. Its connection with Santa Rosalia extends beyond the village to a sanctuary in the wooded surrounding mountains.

Notice: The older town buildings and the change from the Belice's open terrain to a more mountainous setting.

Ride note: Finish town. The sanctuary is outside town; it is not the same destination as your accommodation.

Maps · Santo Stefano Quisquina

03

Teatro Andromeda — a theatre facing the sky

The story: Artist Lorenzo Reina created this stone theatre within the Fattoria dell'Arte. The landscape and sky form part of the experience rather than a painted stage set.

Notice: The stone seating and the way the theatre frames the horizon. This is a contemporary artwork, not an ancient Greek ruin.

Ride note: Optional uphill detour. Check the operator's opening and booking instructions before committing; quiet conduct is required inside.
