Cammarata — the tower above town

The story: The castle was a residence of Cammarata's lords. Much has collapsed, but the tower survives; it later served as a prison into the 1970s.

Notice: How the surviving tower commands the densely built hillside below it.

Ride note: The course passes through town. An interior visit is separate; the municipality warns that opening is irregular.

02

Mussomeli — a castle growing out of rock

The story: The Chiaramonte family established this formidable castle in the 14th century. The isolated rocky setting is as striking as the masonry.

Notice: The transition from natural crag to constructed walls. It makes the defensive logic immediately visible.

Ride note: Mussomeli town is on the stage; the castle stands outside it. Treat a castle visit as a deliberate detour and check access first.

Maps · Castello Manfredonico, Mussomeli

03

Montedoro — beneath the golden name

The story: Sulphur mining shaped local life. The Museo della Zolfara preserves workers' stories and the harsh conditions behind that industry, alongside remains of extraction and processing equipment.

Notice: The contrast between the quiet town today and its industrial past. Ask about the mine museum; do not enter old workings independently.

Ride note: Finish-town option, with museum access to arrange locally.
