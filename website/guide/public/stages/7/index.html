Centuripe — a balcony over the Simeto

The story: Centuripe sits high above the Simeto valley and preserves traces of an ancient Greek and Roman city. Its position makes the surrounding geography part of the attraction.

Notice: The ridge-top townscape and the valley opening around it; look towards Etna when visibility allows.

Ride note: Town-area stop. A climb to a specific viewpoint or museum is optional, not included just because the GPX passes nearby.

Maps · Centuripe town centre

02

Paternò — a Norman lookout

The story: Paternò's Norman castle belongs to the chain of fortifications around Etna. The large, compact keep is very different from Mussomeli's rock-hugging castle.

Notice: The strong rectangular mass rising above the town and plain.

Ride note: Optional hilltop exterior stop. A 15 June 2026 owner notice on Maps says the castle is temporarily closed for safety works; confirm reopening before planning to enter.

Maps · Castello Normanno, Paternò

03

Catania — the lava elephant

The story: Piazza del Duomo brings together Sant'Agata's cathedral and the black lava elephant, Catania's emblem. Volcanic material is part of the city's visual identity, not just its distant backdrop.

Notice: The elephant and the contrasting dark stone and pale façades around the square.

Ride note: Your GPX finish. This is a good place for the end-of-Divide photograph; watch pedestrians with a loaded bike.

Maps · Piazza del Duomo, Catania
