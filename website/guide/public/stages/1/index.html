Palermo — a meeting of cultures

The story: Norman rulers combined Western, Islamic and Byzantine craftsmanship. Palermo's cathedral belongs to the same UNESCO group as Monreale: the mixture, not one pure style, is the point.

Notice: The cathedral's arches and exterior decoration. Save a full interior visit for a less rushed day.

Ride note: Start-city sightseeing; not the pass-pickup address.

02

Monreale — the gold above the valley

The story: This 12th-century cathedral is another expression of Norman Sicily's cultural mix, especially in its extensive mosaics.

Notice: If access and time allow, the mosaic-covered interior; otherwise the cathedral exterior and view from the town above Palermo.

Ride note: A town stop, with extra time needed for the church. Do not let a visit consume your remaining daylight.

Maps · Monreale Cathedral

03

Gibellina Nuova — a town rebuilt as art

The story: After the 1968 Belice earthquake, Gibellina was rebuilt elsewhere with artists and architects helping shape its new identity. Old Gibellina and the new town are different places.

Notice: Pietro Consagra's huge steel star, the town's emblem, and the unusual public spaces around you.

Ride note: Finish-town option. The Cretto belongs to old Gibellina; leave that for Stage 2.
