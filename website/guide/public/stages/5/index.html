Caltanissetta — a fountain and a painted ceiling

The story: Piazza Garibaldi brings together the Triton fountain and Santa Maria la Nova cathedral. Inside, Guglielmo Borremans' frescoes use painted illusion to expand the space overhead.

Notice: The fountain's bronze horse and sea creatures; if the cathedral is open, look up rather than only towards the altar.

Ride note: Unverified intermediate stop until the GPX arrives. Do not reroute here just because it appears in this guide.

Enna — the castle above the island

The story: Castello di Lombardia occupies a commanding medieval stronghold above central Sicily. Its surviving Torre Pisana explains the site's attraction to generations of lookouts.

Notice: The wide agricultural landscape around the ridge. You do not need a full castle visit to understand why height mattered here.

Ride note: Finish-city option after the day's big climb. Check castle and tower access separately.

Maps · Castello di Lombardia, Enna

03

Rocca di Cerere — grain and a goddess

The story: This rock beside the castle is associated with the ancient cult of Demeter, or Ceres to the Romans. The surrounding grain fields make that connection with fertility easy to understand.

Notice: The landscape rather than expecting a surviving complete temple. This is a viewpoint and a place of ancient religious memory.

Ride note: Finish-city walk; mind uneven ground and exposed edges.
