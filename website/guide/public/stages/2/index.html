Cretto di Burri — the missing town

The story: Alberto Burri covered the ruins of old Gibellina with great pale concrete blocks after the 1968 earthquake. This is a memorial made from the town's absence, not an archaeological city.

Notice: The passages between the blocks and the contrast between their geometry and the surrounding hills.

Ride note: Near the course. Walk only where access is permitted; respect barriers and the memorial.

02

Poggioreale Vecchia — life interrupted

The story: The old town was abandoned after the same earthquake. Its surviving streets and damaged buildings show a different response from Gibellina's artwork: the remains of everyday life.

Notice: The old townscape from an authorised viewpoint. Compare it with the deliberately rebuilt towns nearby.

Ride note: Optional stop at the old town, not modern Poggioreale. Never enter unstable buildings or cross a closed gate.

03

Sambuca — seven lanes and a local sweet

The story: The old Arab quarter grew around seven Saracen lanes. Its compact, tangled streets are a sharp contrast to Gibellina Nuova's open modern spaces.

Notice: The lanes around the old quarter, the Rosario church and the climb towards the Chiesa Matrice. Try a local minni di virgini pastry if a bakery has it.

Ride note: Finish-town wander after checking in; walking is easier than hauling the bike through stepped lanes.
