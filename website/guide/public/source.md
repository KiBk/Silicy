## Before and after the ride

**3–4 October:** arrive Catania Saturday around 17:00, collect bikes and transfer to Fausto's place in Modica for two nights. Sunday's Modica–Scicli–Sampieri warm-up is optional, roughly 50 km / 800 m; it is not a checked course.

**13 October:** return Modica → Catania with time for bike handoff and check-in before your girlfriend arrives in the evening. Transport is not yet arranged.

**14 October:** Catania historic centre, fish market, Via Crociferi and a long lunch.

**15 October:** weather-dependent guided Etna day; coast/Taormina fallback. Recheck current access restrictions with the operator; no excursion is booked.

**16 October:** Benedettini, Roman Catania or the lava coast; pack early.

**17 October:** existing plan is a 06:00 flight. Arrange an approximately 03:30 airport transfer and confirm early key return and airline check-in requirements.
