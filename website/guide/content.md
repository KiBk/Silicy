#### Along the stage

Norman Sicily at the start, modern art at the finish. With an afternoon departure, favour brief outdoor stops over museum visits.

##### [Palermo — a meeting of cultures](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1487/)

**The story:** Norman rulers combined Western, Islamic and Byzantine craftsmanship. Palermo's cathedral belongs to the same UNESCO group as Monreale: the mixture, not one pure style, is the point.

**Notice:** The cathedral's arches and exterior decoration. Save a full interior visit for a less rushed day.

**Ride note:** Start-city sightseeing; not the pass-pickup address.

[Maps · Palermo Cathedral](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cattedrale%20di%20Palermo%20Sicilia)

##### [Monreale — the gold above the valley](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1487/)

**The story:** This 12th-century cathedral is another expression of Norman Sicily's cultural mix, especially in its extensive mosaics.

**Notice:** If access and time allow, the mosaic-covered interior; otherwise the cathedral exterior and view from the town above Palermo.

**Ride note:** A town stop, with extra time needed for the church. Do not let a visit consume your remaining daylight.

[Maps · Monreale Cathedral](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cattedrale%20di%20Monreale%20Piazza%20Guglielmo%20II)

##### [Gibellina Nuova — a town rebuilt as art](https://www.italia.it/it/sicilia/gibellina)

**The story:** After the 1968 Belice earthquake, Gibellina was rebuilt elsewhere with artists and architects helping shape its new identity. Old Gibellina and the new town are different places.

**Notice:** Pietro Consagra's huge steel star, the town's emblem, and the unusual public spaces around you.

**Ride note:** Finish-town option. The Cretto belongs to old Gibellina; leave that for Stage 2.

[Maps · Stella di Gibellina](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Stella%20di%20Gibellina%20Ingresso%20al%20Belice)

#### Along the stage

Three responses to history: an earthquake memorial, an abandoned town and a living maze of lanes. Stage 2's exported 65.76 km course differs from the planned 71.36 km; confirm it with Fausto.

##### [Cretto di Burri — the missing town](https://www.visitbelice.it/punti-di-interesse/cretto-di-burri/)

**The story:** Alberto Burri covered the ruins of old Gibellina with great pale concrete blocks after the 1968 earthquake. This is a memorial made from the town's absence, not an archaeological city.

**Notice:** The passages between the blocks and the contrast between their geometry and the surrounding hills.

**Ride note:** Near the course. Walk only where access is permitted; respect barriers and the memorial.

[Maps · Cretto di Burri](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cretto%20di%20Burri%20Gibellina%20Vecchia)

##### [Poggioreale Vecchia — life interrupted](https://www.visitbelice.it/location/comune-di-poggioreale/)

**The story:** The old town was abandoned after the same earthquake. Its surviving streets and damaged buildings show a different response from Gibellina's artwork: the remains of everyday life.

**Notice:** The old townscape from an authorised viewpoint. Compare it with the deliberately rebuilt towns nearby.

**Ride note:** Optional stop at the old town, not modern Poggioreale. Never enter unstable buildings or cross a closed gate.

[Maps · Poggioreale Vecchia](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Poggioreale%20Vecchia)

##### [Sambuca — seven lanes and a local sweet](https://www.visitsicily.info/en/sambuca/)

**The story:** The old Arab quarter grew around seven Saracen lanes. Its compact, tangled streets are a sharp contrast to Gibellina Nuova's open modern spaces.

**Notice:** The lanes around the old quarter, the Rosario church and the climb towards the Chiesa Matrice. Try a local *minni di virgini* pastry if a bakery has it.

**Ride note:** Finish-town wander after checking in; walking is easier than hauling the bike through stepped lanes.

[Maps · Quartiere Saraceno, Sambuca](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Quartiere%20Saraceno%20Sambuca%20di%20Sicilia)

#### Along the stage

The Sicani interior: craft traditions, mountain villages and a very unexpected work of modern art. The theatre is an optional outing, not an assumed stop on the GPX.

##### [Burgio — bells and green ceramics](https://www.visitsicily.info/en/burgio/)

**The story:** Burgio has long traditions of bronze bell-making and decorated majolica. Its ceramics often use green and sandy colours, with flowers and birds among the motifs.

**Notice:** Stone doorways, ceramic details and any open workshops. The ceramics museum is an option if you want more than a quick town stop.

**Ride note:** Town-area stop; workshop and museum access must be checked locally.

[Maps · Burgio town centre](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Burgio%20Sicilia%20centro)

##### [Santo Stefano Quisquina — the mountain pause](https://www.comune.santostefanoquisquina.ag.it/vivere_il_comune/luoghi/luogo_1.html)

**The story:** The town sits in the Sicani mountain landscape. Its connection with Santa Rosalia extends beyond the village to a sanctuary in the wooded surrounding mountains.

**Notice:** The older town buildings and the change from the Belice's open terrain to a more mountainous setting.

**Ride note:** Finish town. The sanctuary is outside town; it is not the same destination as your accommodation.

[Maps · Santo Stefano Quisquina](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Santo%20Stefano%20Quisquina%20centro)

##### [Teatro Andromeda — a theatre facing the sky](https://www.teatroandromeda.it/)

**The story:** Artist Lorenzo Reina created this stone theatre within the Fattoria dell'Arte. The landscape and sky form part of the experience rather than a painted stage set.

**Notice:** The stone seating and the way the theatre frames the horizon. This is a contemporary artwork, not an ancient Greek ruin.

**Ride note:** Optional uphill detour. Check the operator's opening and booking instructions before committing; quiet conduct is required inside.

[Maps · Teatro Andromeda](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Teatro%20Andromeda%20Santo%20Stefano%20Quisquina)

#### Along the stage

Hilltop power, rock-built castles and the labour hidden beneath the landscape. Use Fausto's 68.24 km course, not the longer official variant.

##### [Cammarata — the tower above town](https://www.comune.cammarata.ag.it/vivere_il_comune/luoghi/luogo_8.html)

**The story:** The castle was a residence of Cammarata's lords. Much has collapsed, but the tower survives; it later served as a prison into the 1970s.

**Notice:** How the surviving tower commands the densely built hillside below it.

**Ride note:** The course passes through town. An interior visit is separate; the municipality warns that opening is irregular.

[Maps · Castello di Cammarata](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Castello%20di%20Cammarata%20Via%20Porta%20Pia)

##### [Mussomeli — a castle growing out of rock](https://www.comune.mussomeli.cl.it/vivere_il_comune/territorio/territorio_1.html)

**The story:** The Chiaramonte family established this formidable castle in the 14th century. The isolated rocky setting is as striking as the masonry.

**Notice:** The transition from natural crag to constructed walls. It makes the defensive logic immediately visible.

**Ride note:** Mussomeli town is on the stage; the castle stands outside it. Treat a castle visit as a deliberate detour and check access first.

[Maps · Castello Manfredonico, Mussomeli](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Castello%20Manfredonico%20Mussomeli)

##### [Montedoro — beneath the golden name](https://www.comune.montedoro.cl.it/vivere-il-comune/luoghi/museo-della-zolfara-angelo-petix/)

**The story:** Sulphur mining shaped local life. The Museo della Zolfara preserves workers' stories and the harsh conditions behind that industry, alongside remains of extraction and processing equipment.

**Notice:** The contrast between the quiet town today and its industrial past. Ask about the mine museum; do not enter old workings independently.

**Ride note:** Finish-town option, with museum access to arrange locally.

[Maps · Museo della Zolfara, Montedoro](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Museo%20della%20Zolfara%20Angelo%20Petix%20Montedoro)

#### Along the stage

From sulphur country to Enna's high ridge. Stage 5's GPX is still missing: the intermediate stop below is a regional candidate, not a verified place on Fausto's course.

##### [Caltanissetta — a fountain and a painted ceiling](https://www.visitsicily.info/localita/caltanissetta/)

**The story:** Piazza Garibaldi brings together the Triton fountain and Santa Maria la Nova cathedral. Inside, Guglielmo Borremans' frescoes use painted illusion to expand the space overhead.

**Notice:** The fountain's bronze horse and sea creatures; if the cathedral is open, look up rather than only towards the altar.

**Ride note:** Unverified intermediate stop until the GPX arrives. Do not reroute here just because it appears in this guide.

[Maps · Fontana del Tritone, Caltanissetta](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Fontana%20del%20Tritone%20Piazza%20Garibaldi%20Caltanissetta)

##### [Enna — the castle above the island](https://www.visitsicily.info/localita/enna/)

**The story:** Castello di Lombardia occupies a commanding medieval stronghold above central Sicily. Its surviving Torre Pisana explains the site's attraction to generations of lookouts.

**Notice:** The wide agricultural landscape around the ridge. You do not need a full castle visit to understand why height mattered here.

**Ride note:** Finish-city option after the day's big climb. Check castle and tower access separately.

[Maps · Castello di Lombardia, Enna](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Castello%20di%20Lombardia%20Enna)

##### [Rocca di Cerere — grain and a goddess](https://www.visitsicily.info/localita/enna/)

**The story:** This rock beside the castle is associated with the ancient cult of Demeter, or Ceres to the Romans. The surrounding grain fields make that connection with fertility easy to understand.

**Notice:** The landscape rather than expecting a surviving complete temple. This is a viewpoint and a place of ancient religious memory.

**Ride note:** Finish-city walk; mind uneven ground and exposed edges.

[Maps · Rocca di Cerere, Enna](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Rocca%20di%20Cerere%20Enna)

#### Along the stage

A monumental fountain, a hill town and the country around Regalbuto. Buy Sunday's supplies before settling in; these notes are not a promise that shops will be open.

##### [Leonforte — count the fountain spouts](https://www.ennamagazine.it/Provincia/TabId/100/ArtMID/566/ArticleID/4968/La-Granfonte-Capolavoro-barocco-nel-cuore-della-Sicilia.aspx)

**The story:** The Granfonte is Leonforte's monumental Baroque fountain, nicknamed for its 24 bronze spouts. A basic need—water—became a very public architectural statement.

**Notice:** The repetition of spouts and arches along the long stone frontage.

**Ride note:** A town stop, not a guaranteed drinking-water refill. Check local potability signs before filling bottles.

[Maps · Granfonte, Leonforte](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Granfonte%20Leonforte)

##### [Agira — a castle and a different cassatella](https://visitagira.it/)

**The story:** Agira's houses climb towards the remains of its castle. The town also has its own baked, crescent-shaped cassatella, quite different in appearance from a decorated whole cassata cake.

**Notice:** The stacked townscape; try a cassatella from an open bakery. The castle ruins offer a separate uphill exploration.

**Ride note:** The course reaches Agira, but the upper castle needs extra climbing. A lower-town pastry stop is the easier choice.

[Maps · Agira town centre](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Piazza%20Garibaldi%20Agira%20Sicilia)

##### [Regalbuto — the Pozzillo landscape](https://www.visitsicily.info/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/GUIDA-DEL-DIPORTISTA-vol.2.pdf)

**The story:** Nearby Lago Pozzillo is an artificial reservoir, not a natural mountain lake. Its wooded surroundings add a different landscape to this inland stretch of Sicily.

**Notice:** The wider basin when views allow. A lake-shore visit is a separate outing, not a necessary finish to the ride.

**Ride note:** Finish-town context. Stay in Regalbuto if you would rather eat, resupply and rest; no shoreline access is assumed.

[Maps · Regalbuto town centre](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Regalbuto%20Sicilia%20centro)

#### Along the stage

The long ride south moves into the Hyblean uplands before Modica. Choose brief stops: 112.98 km is already a full day. The GPX starts north of yesterday's Duomo finish; agree how to reach it.

##### [Buccheri — Sicily's old ice trade](https://www.visitsicily.info/en/buccheri/)

**The story:** High in the Hyblean Mountains, Buccheri once stored snow in *neviere* for the ice trade. Refrigeration made that mountain economy obsolete.

**Notice:** In town, Sant'Antonio's dramatic staircase. The surviving snow stores are scattered in the uplands, not all beside the cycling route.

**Ride note:** Town-area stop. Do not add a hunt for remote snow stores to an already long ride.

[Maps · Chiesa di Sant'Antonio Abate, Buccheri](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Chiesa%20Sant%20Antonio%20Abate%20Buccheri)

##### [Giarratana — the quieter Hyblean village](https://www.visitsicily.info/en/itinerario/walking-with-daisy-in-ragusa-land/)

**The story:** The old quarter, called Cuozzu, preserves a compact village setting associated with a route of rural-life house museums. It offers a small-scale contrast to the cities at either end of the day.

**Notice:** The old streets and modest domestic buildings, rather than searching for one huge monument.

**Ride note:** The course passes through Giarratana; exploring the Cuozzu quarter is optional. Check distance and energy before leaving the GPX.

[Maps · Giarratana town centre, by the town hall](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Comune%20di%20Giarratana)

##### [Modica — churches above the streets](https://www.visitsicily.info/itinerario/a-spasso-nel-ragusano/)

**The story:** Modica's dramatic church stairways turn the hillside itself into architecture. San Giorgio and San Pietro are the big visual landmarks, rather than isolated monuments in flat squares.

**Notice:** San Giorgio from below. After a long ride, admiring the staircase is enough—you need not climb every step.

**Ride note:** Finish-city walk. The GPX ends in central Modica, not at Fausto's private address.

[Maps · Duomo di San Giorgio, Modica](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Duomo%20San%20Giorgio%20Modica)

##### [Modica chocolate — why it feels grainy](https://www.visitsicily.info/en/sapore/modica-chocolate/)

**The story:** Low-temperature processing leaves sugar crystals in Modica chocolate, giving it a deliberately grainy texture. That crunch is a feature, not a badly made smooth chocolate bar.

**Notice:** Compare a plain bar with cinnamon or vanilla versions if a shop offers a tasting.

**Ride note:** A relaxed finish-town reward. Shops, tastings and opening hours are not pre-booked.

[Maps · Chocolate shops in central Modica](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=cioccolato%20Corso%20Umberto%20I%20Modica)
