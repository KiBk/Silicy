# Trip plan

Planning baseline: **3–17 October 2026**. All times are local. Items described as candidates are not confirmed bookings.

## 3 October — arrival and transfer to Modica

- Arrive at Catania airport around 17:00.
- Collect the shipped bikes and transfer directly to Modica.
- Use bike-safe accommodation in Modica for both Saturday and Sunday nights.
- Avoid relying on a Sunday rail transfer across the island.

## 4 October — Modica warm-up

Suggested outline: **Modica → Scicli → Sampieri → Modica**, approximately 50 km and 800 m climbing.

This is deliberately flexible. Fausto should choose the quiet local roads; shorten the ride if either bike needs adjustment. Useful pauses are the Duomo di San Giorgio, Via Mormina Penna in Scicli, and the Sampieri seafront.

## 5 October — Modica to Palermo

Ride Fausto's supplied GPX from Modica toward Catania. Leave the GPX at **Catania Centrale**, which it passes shortly before its recorded endpoint.

- Distance to the station: approximately 117.9 km
- Climbing: approximately 1,431 m
- Planned start: 06:00
- Target arrival at station: 15:30–16:00
- Main resupply: Giarratana, Buccheri, and Lentini
- Current train candidate: R 5519, Catania Centrale 17:32 → Palermo Centrale 20:29

The train time and bicycle carriage must be reconfirmed 48–72 hours before departure. If the train becomes a replacement bus that will not take assembled bikes, use a pre-arranged bike shuttle rather than improvising at the station.

## 6–12 October — Sicily Divide

The plan follows the official Palermo variant without compressing stages.

| Date | Stage | Distance | Climbing | Main planning note |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 6 Oct | Palermo → Gibellina | 79 km | 1,394 m | SP12 can become sticky clay after rain; use the official road alternative when wet |
| 7 Oct | Gibellina → Sambuca di Sicilia | 71 km | 1,428 m | View earthquake ruins only from legal, stable access points; carry water |
| 8 Oct | Sambuca → Santo Stefano Quisquina | 59 km | 1,393 m | Do not count on food at San Carlo; resupply in Burgio |
| 9 Oct | Santo Stefano Quisquina → Montedoro | 62 km | 1,479 m | Confirm dinner and bike storage in small Montedoro before departure |
| 10 Oct | Montedoro → Enna | 70 km | 1,705 m | Hardest stage; Caltanissetta is the main food and repair opportunity |
| 11 Oct | Enna → Regalbuto | 63 km | 1,211 m | Sunday opening risk; Leonforte is the strongest planned resupply |
| 12 Oct | Regalbuto → Catania | 61 km | 699 m | Stay alert through the urban finish; arrange bike handoff immediately |

Target the listed destination each day rather than adding evening detours. Every overnight must accept two assembled bikes or provide clearly secure storage.

## 13 October — protected rest and handoff

No riding target. Use the day for recovery, laundry, bike shipping or storage, and any hotel change. Finish the bike administration before the evening reunion, then keep dinner simple around Piazza Duomo or Via Etnea.

## 14 October — Baroque Catania

Walk through Piazza Duomo, La Pescheria, Via Crociferi, and Castello Ursino. Leave space for granita and a long lunch instead of filling every hour.

## 15 October — Mount Etna

Reserve a cancellable guided hike or transfer. Check weather, volcanic access restrictions, altitude, and footwear requirements the day before. Keep the lava coast or Taormina as the fallback if access or weather changes.

## 16 October — culture and lava coast

Visit the Monastero dei Benedettini and Roman theatre, then finish around Aci Castello for the coast and sunset. Pack before dinner because the departure leaves no useful morning margin.

## 17 October — departure

Pre-book a taxi or private transfer for approximately 03:30 and confirm it the day before. Flight departure is planned for 06:00.
