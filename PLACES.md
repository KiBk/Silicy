## Maps and access checks

Google Maps place panels were checked for the cathedrals, Gibellina's Stella, Cretto, Poggioreale Vecchia, Sambuca's Saracen lanes, Teatro Andromeda, Cammarata tower, Mussomeli castle, Montedoro mine museum, Caltanissetta's Triton fountain, Enna castle and Rocca di Cerere, Granfonte, Agira's Piazza Garibaldi, Paternò castle, Buccheri's Sant'Antonio and Modica's San Giorgio. Town queries were checked for Burgio, Santo Stefano Quisquina, Regalbuto, Centuripe and Catania's Piazza del Duomo.

- Replaced an ambiguous Poggioreale search with **Poggioreale Vecchia**, resolving to the ghost town rather than modern Poggioreale.
- Burgio's old-town search also matched accommodation names; its button uses the checked town query instead.
- Giarratana's broad old-town search returned multiple results. Its button now explicitly opens the **town centre by the town hall**, Piazza Vittorio Veneto 3; it does not pretend to identify a single Cuozzu entrance.
- Modica's chocolate link is intentionally a category search, not a selected or endorsed business.
- Paternò castle's Maps owner notice dated **15 June 2026** says it is temporarily closed for safety works. The guide recommends an exterior stop and checking for reopening; displayed generic hours are not proof of access.
- No museum, church, theatre, shop, tasting or detour is booked or guaranteed open. Granfonte is not promised as potable water. Ruins, mine workings and closed gates are not invitations to enter.

Maps buttons locate sights; the GPX remains the cycling route. Stage 1's endpoints were checked as public streets before publishing its unchanged file. No private home address or live tracking is added.
